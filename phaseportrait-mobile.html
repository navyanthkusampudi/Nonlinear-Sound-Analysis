On mobile, use Show shared controls in the Spectrogram tab to set the common frame window, tau, and PCA dimension used by all plots. Use one finger on a 3D plot to rotate, two fingers to pinch-zoom and drag/pan, and the right-side scroll strip to move up/down the page.
