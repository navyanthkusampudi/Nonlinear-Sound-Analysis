Sound as a Dynamical Link

Two systems begin independently. A signal creates a coupling channel that can coordinate their dynamics. The same sound is shown as a waveform, spectrogram, and a 2D attractor.
Coupling strength K = 0.80
Attractor delay τ = 18
How to use: 1) Tap START MIC. 2) Say “aaaa” or “ooo”. 3) Watch the waveform, spectrogram, and attractor update live. 4) Cut or restore the connection between the two agents. 5) Turn VOICE CONTROL ON to let speech energy control coupling.
The demo can run without microphone access, but the live sound panels need microphone permission.
