What you are seeing

This interactive demonstration converts a hand-drawn pressure–tension gesture into synthetic birdsong, then reveals the resulting signal’s time-delay dynamics.

1 · Draw

The upper-left plot represents pressure horizontally and labial tension vertically. The stroke becomes time-varying controls α(t) and β(t).

2 · Generate sound

Two left/right syrinx ODEs are integrated with RK4, their outputs mixed, then filtered by the trachea and oro-oesophageal cavity (OEC).

3 · Explore dynamics

The right plots show pressure/tension histories, the resulting spectrogram and waveform. Below-left, the moving golden phase-space trajectory represents the selected time window.

The vertical playheads indicate the same position in the generated song. Use Replay, Pause, the Speed slider, or the Time scrubber to inspect the dynamics without altering the simulated audio.

Model equations

Bilateral labial oscillator

For each side of the syrinx, x is labial displacement and y is its velocity. The pressure drive α(t), tension parameter β(t), and stiffness/time-scale γ determine the motion.

ẋ = y
ẏ = −α(t)γ2 − β(t)γ2x − γ2x3 − γx2y + γ2x2 − γxy
α(t): pressure drive from the drawn gesture
β(t): labial tension from the drawn gesture
γ: model time-scale / stiffness parameter
x(t), y(t): labial displacement and velocity

Tracheal delay and reflection

The mixed syringeal source passes through a tracheal delay/reflection stage:

Pin(t) = ν(t)xmix(t) − rPin(t − T)
Ptr(t) = (1 − r)Pin(t − T/2)
T = 2L/c

Here L is trachea length, c is sound speed, r is the reflection coefficient, and ν(t) is the airflow/mean-flow proxy. The OEC stage then shapes the spectrum, using a browser-stable second-order Helmholtz filter with resonance frequency f₀ and quality factor Q by default. An advanced three-state OEC model is also selectable.

Time-delay reconstruction

A measured scalar signal s[n]—by default the final synthesized sound—is embedded using delayed copies of itself. For dimension m and delay k samples:

zn = [s[n], s[n − k], …, s[n − (m − 1)k]]
k ≈ τfs    (τ in seconds; rounded to ≥ 1 sample)

The gold line and points trace these coordinates through time. The illuminated point marks the latest state; only the chosen sliding time window is emphasized. With m = 2, the embedding is plotted in 2D; with m = 3, it is shown in 3D. For m > 3, principal component analysis (PCA) projects the m-dimensional embedding onto its first three principal components.

In Settings, you can reconstruct the original generated samples without resampling, display all trajectory points, switch line/scatter modes, or independently adjust line opacity and scatter opacity. The time-delay animation speed is independent of the original sound playback.

The syrinx and trachea → OEC architecture follows the cited birdsong-model literature. The interactive f₀/Q Helmholtz filter is a simplified browser-oriented stage rather than a claim to reproduce a full anatomical vocal tract; the advanced three-state OEC mode is provided separately.
